About me

More than a diploma –
responsibility to the patient

Martin Dvořák, born in 1988 in Prague, has loved sport since childhood. At twenty he chose fitness as a path to health and physical fitness, while completing the Faculty of Medicine at Charles University, majoring in Vocational Physiotherapy.

Through years of continuous training – including a specialization in the lumbar spine at renowned institutions abroad – he has built an approach that combines osteopathy, manual therapy and modern physiotherapy.

  • Master of Physiotherapy – Charles University
  • Osteopath, lumbar spine specialist
  • Licensed IFBB bodybuilding and operational fitness trainer
  • Specialized education at international institutions
  • Individual approach: examination, diagnosis and therapy plan for every patient

Therapies and treatments

Complete range of services

Every therapy is individually tailored – no templated approach, with expert assessment and progress monitoring.

Physiotherapy
Osteopathy and manual spine therapy

A complete examination of the spine, manipulative techniques, chiropractic and an individual recovery plan.

  • Neck, back and lower back pain
  • Sciatica and herniated disc
  • Deep myofascial technique
Physical therapy
Vacuum (cupping) therapy

Specialized medical devices work deeply – they improve circulation, reduce muscle tension and speed up recovery.

  • Quick relief from pain and tension
  • Improves local circulation
  • Speeds up tissue regeneration
Premium therapy
Manual lymphatic drainage

A sophisticated therapy that speeds up recovery, reduces swelling and restores a feeling of lightness and vitality.

  • Tissue preparation before surgery
  • Faster reduction of swelling after surgery
  • Detoxification of the body
Modern method
Dry needling therapy

A modern, proven method that quickly and effectively relieves back and muscle pain and chronic complaints.

  • Relaxation of deep muscles
  • Reduced pain and inflammation
  • Increased range of motion
Thermotherapy
Fango thermal therapy

Natural mineral muds combined with heat – effectively reduce pain and stiffness and speed up regeneration.

  • Chronic and acute pain
  • Improves joint mobility
  • Natural mineral composition
Relaxation
Volcanic stone massage

Warm volcanic stones relax muscles, reduce pain, improve circulation and restore the energy of body and mind.

  • Deep muscle relaxation
  • Stress and chronic fatigue
  • Improved circulation
Aesthetic physiotherapy
Maderotherapy – anti-cellulite

Visible reduction of cellulite, improved skin tone and natural detoxification of the body through expertly guided therapy.

  • Breakdown of fat deposits
  • Body contouring
  • Combined with lymphatic drainage
Massage
Relax massage

Not just a massage – but relief, release and a return of energy. Ideal for stress, tension and everyday fatigue.

  • Tension in the back and neck
  • Stress and mental strain
  • General regeneration of the body
Pediatric physiotherapy
Corrective exercise for children

Expertly guided programs for correct posture, correction of scoliosis and hyperkyphosis – individual and group work.

  • Poor posture and scoliosis
  • Adapted to age and needs
  • Motivating environment
Rehabilitation
Joint rehabilitation and sports injuries

Knee, ankle, elbow – recovery from injuries and post-operative rehabilitation with a safe return to activity.

  • Reduced pain and swelling
  • Muscle strengthening and proprioception
  • Prevention of re-injury
Kinesitherapy
Physiotherapy exercises and kinesitherapy

Active and passive exercises adapted to the patient's condition – before and after surgery, as well as in the prevention of chronic pain.

  • Pre- and post-operative preparation
  • Strengthening of spinal stabilizers
  • Prevention of degenerative changes
Neurorehabilitation
Polyneuropathy and fibromyalgia

Individually tailored therapy for patients with chronic pain, tingling and reduced limb function.

  • Return of sensation in the limbs
  • Reduced tingling and burning
  • Improved quality of life
